How it works

From application to harvest — the 7-step lifecycle of a fostered coffee tree.

7-step lifecycle

STEP 1

Submit fostering application

Individuals or enterprises apply via web/app. Select plan (single/multi/batch), fill in shipping address and reward preference (beans / cash / mixed). Enterprise users add ESG reporting requirements.

STEP 2

Smart matching

System matches fosterer with an available aeroponic cultivator at a venue (hotel / cafe / farm). Matching considers device capacity, geography, varietal fit, and expected carbon reduction.

STEP 3 — FOSTERING PERIOD (6-18 MONTHS)

Real-time reduction trajectory

Three data streams pushed to fosterer: carbon reduction trajectory (daily/weekly/monthly CO₂), growth visualization (timelapse + biomass), and community interaction (messages, achievements, leaderboard).

STEP 4

Harvest & processing

Cherries harvested by venue operator. System records batch. Processing includes dehulling, roasting (light/medium/dark), and packaging with fosterer-exclusive carbon label + QR traceability code.

STEP 5

Outcome distribution

Channel A (beans): roasted beans shipped to fosterer. Channel B (cash): beans enter market, 40% revenue auto-settled to wallet. Channel C (carbon): credits + ESG report delivered.

STEP 6

Hotel proxy shipping

Hotel attributes in-stay carbon reduction to guest account. On checkout, hotel ships beans to guest's home/company with a personalized carbon certificate.

STEP 7

Renew, gift, or close

Fosterer renews for next cycle (priority match to same device), transfers fostering rights as a gift card, or closes. Closing triggers a full-cycle carbon reduction summary report.

The 1st World Aeroponic Coffee Grower

A breakthrough in sustainable agriculture — growing coffee without soil, using nutrient-rich mist to cultivate plants in air. This is the technology behind every gram of CO₂ sequestered in the Regal Genesis Chronicle network.

About the technology

Aeroponic cultivation represents a paradigm shift in coffee agriculture.

☁

Air-based growing

Roots suspended in air, misted with nutrient solution every 3-5 minutes. No soil, no runoff, 95% less water than traditional cultivation.

💧

95% water savings

Traditional coffee farming uses 18,000 L water per kg of beans. Aeroponic systems use under 900 L — a 95% reduction.

🌱

Year-round growth

Controlled environment eliminates seasonal constraints. Coffee trees grow 30% faster with 2x higher yield density per m².

⚡

Low energy

Each device consumes only 0.2-0.3 kWh/day. Solar-compatible. Energy cost offset by carbon credit revenue.

Why this matters

Coffee is one of the most carbon-intensive agricultural commodities. Traditional supply chains generate 8.3 kg CO₂ per kg of roasted coffee. By growing coffee at the point of consumption — in homes, hotels, and cafes — we eliminate transport, reduce processing, and turn coffee from a carbon source into a carbon sink.
